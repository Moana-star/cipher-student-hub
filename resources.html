Study methods

Try a different way to study.

Use these three methods to review your notes, test your memory, and plan your study time.

Start here

Three methods to try

Write a short summary

Close your notes and write the main idea in two or three sentences. Reopen them and correct anything you missed.

Use a timer

Study for 25 minutes, then take a five-minute break. If 25 minutes feels too long, start with ten.

Test yourself

Answer a few questions without looking at your notes. Check your mistakes, review the topic, and try again.
