Study tips

Summarise your notes

Close your notes and write the main points in your own words. Check your notes to see what you missed.

Use a timer

Study for 25 minutes, then take a five-minute break. Start with ten minutes if that works better for you.

Test yourself

Try a few questions without looking at your notes. Check your answers and review anything you got wrong.
