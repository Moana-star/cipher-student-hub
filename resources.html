Notes, practice, repeat

Tools for your next study session.

You do not need to try every technique at once. Pick one idea from this page, test it during your next session, and keep it if it helps.

Things worth trying

Make your study time work harder.

Summarise without copying

Close the book after a section and write two or three sentences about the main point. Check the original afterwards and fill in what you missed.

Give yourself a stopping point

Try 25 minutes of focused work followed by a five-minute break. If that feels too long, start with ten minutes and adjust from there.

Practise before you feel ready

Answer a few questions from memory. Getting one wrong is useful information: review the reason, then try a similar question again.
