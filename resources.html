A few good habits

Build your own study routine.

✎

Write short notes

After a lesson, write the main idea in your own words. It is a useful way to check what you understood.

◷

Take a real break

Try studying for 25 minutes, then take a five-minute break. Adjust the timing to what works for you.

✓

Practise regularly

Answer a few questions without looking at your notes, then review any mistakes calmly.
