STUDY NOTES01
One task at a time.

A routine that can work

You do not need a perfect study day.

A useful session can be as simple as understanding one difficult idea or finishing a few practice questions. Pick a task you can actually complete, then build on it.

01 / CHOOSE

Decide what matters today

Look at your deadlines and choose one priority. If the task feels too big, break it into smaller pieces.

02 / TRY

Put the notes away

After reading, explain the idea in your own words or solve a question without checking the answer first.

03 / CHECK

Notice what is still unclear

Mark the parts that confused you. Those are good places to start next time, or to ask someone about.

Keep this in mind

“Progress can be quiet: one question answered, one page reviewed, one thing understood.”

Small steps count, too.
