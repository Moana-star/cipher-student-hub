STUDY NOTES01
One task at a time.

A simple place to start

Focus on one task at a time.

Choose a task you can finish in the time you have. You can plan the next one when you are done.

01 / CHOOSE

Pick one thing

Check what is due soon, then choose one task. “Review chapter two” is easier to start than “study everything.”

02 / TRY

Test your memory

Close your notes and try to explain the idea out loud, or answer one question from memory. Then check what you missed.

03 / CHECK

Leave yourself a note

Write down what still confuses you so you know what to review next time.

Need a reset?

Try ten focused minutes.

Put your phone aside and work on one question or one page. After ten minutes, decide what to do next.
