STUDY NOTES01
One task at a time.

Start where you are

A study session does not have to go perfectly.

Maybe you have twenty minutes before class, or an hour to catch up. Choose something small enough to finish in the time you have. A completed practice question is better than an ambitious plan you never start.

01 / CHOOSE

Pick one thing

Check what is due soon, then choose one task. “Review chapter two” is easier to start than “study everything.”

02 / TRY

Test your memory

Close your notes and try to explain the idea out loud, or answer one question from memory. Then check what you missed.

03 / CHECK

Leave yourself a note

Before you stop, write down the bit that still feels confusing. You will know exactly where to pick up next time.

If you lose focus

Make the next step smaller.

Get a glass of water, put your phone out of reach, and choose one question or one page. You can reassess after ten minutes. You do not need to rescue the whole day at once.
