Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what happens when you visit Cypher Hub, a student learning project. The site provides study suggestions, learning resources, browser-based planning tools, quizzes and flashcards.

Information you enter

The contact page is a demonstration form only. It is not connected to a message service and is not designed to send messages to the site owner. Do not enter sensitive or confidential information into the form.

Information collected by the site

This project does not include its own account system, analytics, advertising trackers, or a server database for visitor submissions. Study planner tasks and flashcard progress are stored in your browser using local storage on the device you use. They are not synced to an account and may be lost if browser site data is cleared. The contact form does not send or store submissions. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical information such as request logs to provide and protect its hosting service. Refer to GitHub's privacy statement for details about its processing.

Links to other websites

The resources page links to third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices and terms. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

Personal information

Please do not submit passwords, financial details, identification numbers, health information, or other sensitive personal data through this learning project.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website's features change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.

Contact

This is a student project and no dedicated contact service is configured on this site. The contact form is not monitored.
